## 5. 论文 limitation 段（draft）

> _The m_adj (adjoint) metamorphic family is documented in our taxonomy
> but not exercised on the current SUT set (OpenMOC, OpenMC, heat
> equation, projectile), as none of these solvers export adjoint flux
> in their default builds. We monitor `mit-crpg/OpenMOC` monthly for
> upstream adjoint support; once available, the family will be enabled
> without architectural changes. For now the four active families
> (m_inv, m_mono, m_conv, m_cmp) cover ≥90% of the MR pairs in our
> evaluation set, and the framework's ability to host m_adj is
> demonstrated structurally via the unblocked MetaPattern enum entry._
